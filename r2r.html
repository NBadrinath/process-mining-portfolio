Record-to-Report (R2R)

A slow close usually isn't a headcount problem — it's a process variant problem.

Manual journal entries, reconciliation lag, and inconsistent close calendars quietly stretch the close every single period. Mining the close process shows exactly which task is the critical path.

Where the value hides

Three patterns that show up in almost every R2R event log

Each one is a distinct, replayable path variant once close-task data is mined against actual timestamps.

01 · Rework

Manual journal entries

A high share of manual entries versus automated postings drives both close duration and error/rework risk — and it's rarely evenly distributed across entities.

02 · Delay

Reconciliation bottlenecks

Subledger-to-GL reconciliation lagging behind the close calendar is often the single biggest driver of late close, especially for intercompany accounts.

03 · Variance

Inconsistent close calendars

Different entities or regions following different close sequences make it hard to see where the group-level bottleneck actually sits.

Share of close tasks completed without rework or reopening — the clearest signal of close process stability.

Ranges are typical benchmarks observed across similar R2R landscapes, not a guarantee for any specific client — actual movement depends on your process maturity, ERP configuration, and data quality.

How I approach it

From raw close-task logs to a prioritized fix list

01

Extract & model the event log

Pull journal entry, task-management, and reconciliation tool data and reconstruct the true close-cycle case structure across entities.

02

Build the KPI tree

Define close duration, manual entry rate, and reconciliation-lag KPIs in PQL, validated against controllership's own close reporting.

03

Surface deviations & root causes

Replay the close to find which entity, account group, or task owner is consistently on the critical path — not just that the close runs long.

04

Quantify business impact

Translate each deviation into close-days and rework hours, so the fix list is ranked by value, not noise.

05

Prioritize & support the fix

Work with controllership to pilot task automation or resequencing, then track the KPI tree across the next close to confirm it moved.
