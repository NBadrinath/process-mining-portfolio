Where the value hides

Three patterns that show up in almost every O2C event log

Each one shows up as a distinct, replayable path variant once the order flow is mined end to end.

01 · Delay

Order blocks

Credit holds, pricing errors, or missing master data stall orders before they even reach fulfillment — often for reasons that have nothing to do with the customer.

02 · Rework

Late or partial deliveries

Delivery performance issues ripple downstream into disputes and deductions, which then drag out the invoice-to-cash step even further.

03 · Leakage

Manual invoice corrections

Pricing or quantity errors caught late force invoice reissues, adding days to DSO and creating reconciliation headaches for the customer's AP team.

How I approach it

From raw order tables to a prioritized fix list

01

Extract & model the event log

Pull sales order, delivery, and billing tables and reconstruct the true order-to-cash case structure, including every block and re-release.

02

Build the KPI tree

Define DSO, block frequency, and delivery-performance KPIs in PQL, validated against finance and logistics' own reporting.

03

Surface deviations & root causes

Replay the process to find which customer, product line, plant, or block type is driving the delay — not just that delays exist.

04

Quantify business impact

Translate each deviation into days-of-DSO and euros of working capital, so the fix list is ranked by value, not noise.

05

Prioritize & support the fix

Work with sales, logistics, and billing owners to pilot the top interventions, then track DSO post-change to confirm it moved.
