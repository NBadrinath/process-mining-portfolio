Where the value hides

Three patterns that show up in almost every P2P event log

These aren't hypothetical — they surface the moment you replay the process against the actual timestamps.

01 · Leakage

Maverick buying

Purchases made outside approved contracts or preferred vendors, missing negotiated pricing entirely. Usually invisible until you trace PO creation against catalog and contract data.

02 · Rework

Three-way match failures

PO, goods receipt, and invoice disagree on price or quantity, kicking the invoice into manual exception handling and delaying payment (or discount capture).

03 · Delay

Approval bottlenecks

POs sitting in an approver's queue well past SLA, especially once a request is routed for exception approval — often the single biggest driver of cycle time variance.

KPIs this engagement moves

The metrics a steering committee actually tracks

Touchless invoice rate

35%→70%

Share of invoices that post without manual intervention. The clearest signal of how much match-failure rework is happening upstream.

Maverick buying spend

20%→8%

Percentage of total spend routed outside approved contracts or catalogs — direct, quantifiable savings once curbed.

PO-to-payment cycle time

22 days→12 days

End-to-end duration from PO creation to payment clearing, broken down by the stage actually causing the delay.

Early payment discount capture

<20%→60%+

Share of eligible invoices paid inside the discount window — often left on the table purely due to approval delay.

Ranges are typical benchmarks observed across similar P2P landscapes, not a guarantee for any specific client — actual movement depends on your process maturity, ERP configuration, and data quality.

How I approach it

From raw ERP tables to a prioritized fix list

01

Extract & model the event log

Pull PO, GR, and invoice tables from SAP/Oracle/your ERP and reconstruct the true case-activity-timestamp structure — the foundation everything else depends on.

02

Build the KPI tree

Define touchless rate, cycle time, and spend-compliance KPIs in PQL, validated against finance's own numbers so nobody argues with the dashboard.

03

Surface deviations & root causes

Replay the process to find which vendor, buyer, plant, or approval path is driving the exceptions — not just that exceptions exist.

04

Quantify business impact

Translate each deviation into euros: discount missed, rework hours, working-capital drag — so the fix list is ranked by value, not noise.

05

Prioritize & support the fix

Work with process owners to pilot the top interventions, then track the KPI tree post-change to confirm it actually moved.
